How The System Works

A seamless end-to-end pipeline from desktop configuration to physical mobile AR tracking.

01

Select or Upload Model

Pick from built-in military & sci-fi assets or upload your own custom .glb / .gltf 3D file.

02

Configure in 3D

Tune scale, height above marker, offsets, and rotation in the real-time OrbitControls studio.

03

Generate Dual QR

Creates a high-contrast QR code that encodes the experience URL and serves as the physical AR tracking marker.

04

Scan & Experience AR

Scan with your mobile camera. Point at the printed QR code and view the 3D model from 360° in real physical space.
